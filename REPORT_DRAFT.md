## How it works

**Input.** Everything uses the new Input System. The actions are made in code with `InputAction` (Space and Up arrow to jump, Shift to dash, Esc or P to pause). The menu buttons work through `InputSystemUIInputModule`.

**Classes.** `Interactable` is the base class for everything that comes at the player. `Obstacle` extends it and removes a life on contact. `GroundObstacle` is the cactus and `AirObstacle` is the bird. `StarItem` extends `Interactable` directly and gives 50 points. The player just calls `OnPlayerContact` on whatever it touches.

**Gameplay.** The player has 3 lives, shown with a health bar. A hit takes one life and the player can't be hit again for 0.8 seconds. At 0 lives the game is over. Dash makes the world move twice as fast for a short time and then has a cooldown.

**Animator.** The states are Run, Jump, Hit and Death. Run and Jump switch on a `Grounded` bool, and Hit and Death are triggers. The Hit clip also flashes the sprite red for a moment.

**Background.** Clouds, mountains, hills and ground scroll at different speeds (`ParallaxLayer`), so the far ones look further away.

**UI.** There is a main menu, a pause menu and a game over screen. The current score and high score are always shown while playing.

**High score.** Saved with `PlayerPrefs` when the game ends, on restart, and when quitting.

**Extra.** All the pixel art is generated by code. The bird flaps, the speed goes up the longer you survive, and stars can be collected for bonus points.
